Instructions

Click Insert 20 gp to start the machine, the reels will start to spin, to stop the reel on the selected monster click Pull Lever. Each pull stops the leftmost spinning reel on the symbol it is showing, and the next reel starts spinning. When the fourth reel stops, the four lights give feedback:

Green — a symbol is correct and on the correct reel.
Blue — a symbol is correct but on the wrong reel.
Dark — no match.

The lights don’t say which reels they refer to. Insert another 20 gp to try again; every attempt is recorded in the history panel above the reels.
